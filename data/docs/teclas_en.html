Keys and buttons of Puerto Bravo

Full list of the game's keys. It comes from the program itself, so it is always up to date. Inside the game you have it with F1.

In menus

  • Up Arrow and Down Arrow: move through the options.
  • Enter: choose.
  • Left arrow and right arrow: change the value of an option.
  • Escape: go back. Every menu also has a Go back option.
  • Home and End: first and last option.
  • A letter: jumps to the next option that starts with it. Accents don't count: A also goes to what starts with Á.
  • Delete, in Adjust the volume of each sound: returns the chosen sound to its standard volume. With a gamepad, it is done from the Changed sounds list in that same menu.
  • F1: explanation of the current option.
  • Page Up and Page Down: review the previous messages.
  • Tab, when typing a text: goes through the suggested phrases or names, if there are any, and leaves the chosen one written. Shift Tab goes back to the previous one.

In story dialogues, Enter moves on to the next line, Up Arrow repeats it, Left Arrow goes back to the previous one and Escape skips the dialogue.

Any time

  • C: where am I: street, neighborhood and which way you are facing. With Shift it adds the coordinates.
  • V: status: health, money, weapon, wanted level and vehicle.
  • T: time, day and weather.
  • O: objective of the current mission or job: it first says where it is, how many meters away, in which direction and, if it is moving, where it is going, and then the rest. If the objective moves or time is running, one press says only that, and two in a row read the whole explanation. In a heist with your gang it also says the phase and who does what; with a commercial flight or a hijacked aircraft, where you have to take it.
  • Shift O: go back to the objective of the mission or job: it drops any detour, sets it on the GPS and, if it is within range, selects it on the radar. It always does the same thing.
  • N: description of the place or neighborhood you are in.
  • Shift N: next intersection: which street is the next one you will cross in the direction you are facing or driving, and how many meters away it is.
  • Shift V: only hunger, thirst, sleep and hygiene, with realistic life turned on.
  • Shift L: where the vehicle you got out of is: it says so and marks it on the GPS. If you were heading somewhere else, when you reach the vehicle the GPS goes back to that destination by itself. If you have more vehicles, it says how many: they are on the phone, in My vehicles.
  • K: which way you are facing or driving, and plays a tone from the north to orient you.
  • F10: when playing with friends or on a server, type a message: it is read aloud to the others. To open your game to your friends or join one of theirs, open the phone with M and go into Play with friends.
  • Shift F10: on a server with roleplay channels, speak in character: only those near you hear it.
  • Ctrl F10: on a server with roleplay channels, describe what your character does: you type "opens the door" and those nearby hear your name and "opens the door".
  • F9: map explorer: moves through the town with a cursor, intersection by intersection, without moving the character. Tab says the places next to the cursor and Enter sets the destination.
  • Tab and Shift Tab: radar: sweeps what is around you, from nearest to farthest.
  • B: sets the GPS and the beacon toward the last thing the radar said. If you were already going somewhere, it is a detour: when you get into that vehicle, reach that person, reach that object or pick it up, or reach that place, the GPS goes back to what you had, and says so.
  • F6: changes the radar filter: everything, places, vehicles, people or objects.
  • L: list of places in the city to set a destination.
  • G: repeats the GPS instruction. In a mission, with no destination set, it sets again what the mission asks; with no mission, it recovers the last destination you removed.
  • Shift G: removes the GPS destination. If you were on a detour, the GPS goes back to what you had; another press removes that too.
  • Ctrl G: recovers the last destination or detour you removed with Shift G, without having to look for it again. If you are now going somewhere else, it enters as a detour.
  • M: opens the phone: missions, places, map explorer, bookmarks, services, skills, achievements, properties and statistics. Life, reputation and consequences is where you find how the city sees you, how the street sees you and what you gain or lose because of it, with your record, your contract, your studies, your businesses and what you are going to be charged.
  • Enter: interact: enter a place, start a mission, pick up an object or talk.
  • F: get into a vehicle or out of it.
  • Shift F: on a server, pull another player out of their vehicle and take it: they have to be going slowly and be next to you.
  • Q: locks onto the nearest enemy; press it again to move to the next one. Shift Q locks onto anyone.
  • Numbers 1 to 9: choose a weapon. Comma goes to the next one and Shift comma to the previous one.
  • I: opens the list of your weapons so you can pick one.
  • P: at sea, casts the fishing rod; when you hear a bite, press Space. In emergency vehicles, turns on the siren.
  • Z: uses a first aid kit.
  • Shift H: surrender: with the police after you and an officer nearby, you raise your hands. Officers stop shooting and come to arrest you; do not move or attack. The higher the wanted level, the bigger the fine and the more hours in a cell, and at the highest level you lose all your weapons. Another press lowers your hands.
  • Page Up and Page Down: go back over earlier messages. Period repeats the latest one.
  • Ctrl Page Up and Ctrl Page Down: go to the first message in the history and to the last one. In Options, Voice, you choose whether each new message takes you to the end.
  • F1: this help.
  • F2: switches between assisted and free driving.
  • F3: switches walking between compass and turning.
  • F4: turns the GPS sound beacon on or off.
  • F5: quick save, outside missions.
  • F7 and F8: lower and raise the music volume.
  • F12: puts a marker in the session log. Press it when you notice something odd: that way, whoever reviews the log will know when it happened.
  • Escape: pause menu.

On foot

  • Arrow keys: walk. In compass mode each arrow goes toward a cardinal point: up is north, right is east. In turning mode, left and right turn 45 degrees and up moves forward.
  • Left Shift with the arrow keys: run. The right Shift key does not make you run or switch between walking and running. Combinations, like Shift N, work with either one and do not change the pace.
  • W, A, S and D: directional scanner: it says what is ahead, to the left, behind and to the right, and how far away the wall is.
  • Space: attack or shoot. With automatic weapons, hold it down.
  • R: reload.
  • Shift R: the music player with headphones, if you bought it: it turns it on and changes station, so you can hear the radio and your music on foot. One of the stations is off.
  • X: crouch or stand up. When crouching you are hit less, but you move slower.
  • Shift X: take cover behind a wall or a parked vehicle, or leave it. In cover you are hit much less; if you move, you leave cover.
  • Backspace: slow time: everything goes slower while you have concentration left. Another press turns it off.
  • H: shout to scare off pedestrians. On duty as a police officer, orders suspects to stop.
  • E: with a weapon in your hand, rob the pedestrian next to you.
  • Y: if you have a dog, you ask it to sniff out the nearest object.
  • Shift Y: your dog's menu: how it is, pet it, feed it, send it home or call it.
  • J: talk to the person next to you. Each one has their own name, job and personality: you can greet them, ask about a place, invite them for a coffee, make friends or type whatever you want to tell them.
  • Shift J: when playing with friends or on a server, says how the nearest player is dressed: their clothes and accessories. On a server with roleplay channels, it also reads their character sheet, if they wrote one. It only says it when you ask.
  • U: walk by itself: with a destination set on the GPS, the character follows the route on its own until arriving. Hold Shift to make it run. Any arrow key, or U again, stops it.

At the wheel, on a boat and in a helicopter

  • Up arrow: accelerate.
  • Down arrow: brake and, once stopped, reverse.
  • Space: handbrake.
  • Left and right arrows: in assisted driving they signal: the car will turn by itself at the next intersection. In free driving they turn the wheel.
  • A and D: in assisted driving, hold them to change lanes: A takes the opposite lane to overtake and D pulls you to the curb. Flying a helicopter or a light plane, with real flight, D goes up and A goes down.
  • A quick tap of A: the GPS autopilot settings: turn it on or off and choose how it drives: Automatic, Calm, Normal obeying the rules, Hurried but obeying the rules, Hurried breaking the rules, or Super hurried, at full speed.
  • Automatic braking: in assisted driving the car brakes by itself if there is a vehicle ahead in your lane. It is turned off in Options.
  • U: U-turn, with the car almost stopped. With the GPS autopilot, it does it by itself when the destination is behind you.
  • Y: GPS autopilot: the car goes by itself to the set destination; it accelerates, brakes, turns, waits at traffic lights and stops on arrival.
  • E: in free driving, straightens the car toward the nearest cardinal point.
  • W: current speed.
  • H: horn.
  • R: changes the station. Shift R turns the radio off.
  • Ctrl: shoot from the vehicle without getting out, with a firearm in your hand: first Q locks onto whoever is near, and Ctrl shoots them. I changes weapon. With an automatic weapon, hold it down.
  • J: starts or ends a job with the vehicle: taxi, ambulance, bus, patrol car or truck. If you are a police officer, in a patrol car it starts or ends the shift.
  • Helicopter: and light plane: they fly above buildings and traffic and are flown by hand, like a boat. With real flight, up arrow takes off, D goes up, A goes down and a note says the altitude; to land, brake and press F. When you reach the edge of the town, or with Enter at a heliport or an airport, you choose a destination and make the crossing: there, the arrows control heading and altitude, Tab is the autopilot, Enter says how the flight is going, N speeds it up and F1 explains it. With real flight turned off in Options, you always fly at the same height and Enter at a heliport takes you in one jump.
  • At sea: boats are always steered by hand: left and right turn the helm and the heading is announced. Enter next to a pier docks the boat.

Walking around inside a house, a venue or a shop

  • Left arrow and right arrow: go to the room, hall or corridor next door. You hear your footsteps toward that side and the game says where you arrive.
  • Enter: use the place where you are. In your home kitchen, put away the groceries, open the fridge or the pantry and cook. In a food shop aisle, see its products: Enter or right arrow puts one in the basket and left arrow puts it back. At the register, pay for the basket.
  • C: in a food shop, what you have in the basket and how much it adds up to. With a controller, X or Square.
  • F1: where you are, what Enter does there and where you have to go to be served.
  • Escape: leave. In a food shop, with something in the basket, it first asks what you do with it: pay, leave it, take it without paying or keep shopping.

Gamepad button names are the Xbox ones. With a PlayStation controller the game says them with their own names: cross, circle, square and triangle.

Gamepad, in menus

  • In menus: D-pad or left stick to move.
  • A or Cross to choose.
  • B or Circle to go back.
  • Y or Triangle for help on the option.
  • X or Square to repeat.
  • LB and RB, or L1 and R1, to review earlier messages.
  • On a server's waiting screen: A or Cross repeats the status, B or Circle stops waiting, and Y or Triangle explains it.
  • On a flight crossing: D-pad up and down, altitude; D-pad left and right, heading; X or Square, autopilot; A or Cross, flight status; Y or Triangle, speed up; LB or L1, repeat the tower message; RB or R1, or B or Circle, the flight menu; and the View, Create or Share button, help.
  • Browsing inside a food shop: X or Square tells you what's in your basket. In each aisle, A or Cross, or D-pad right, puts the product in the basket, and D-pad left puts it back.
  • To type text with the gamepad: D-pad up and down cycle through the letters, D-pad right types the chosen letter and D-pad left deletes the last one. LB or L1 adds a space. RB or R1 switches between lowercase, uppercase, numbers and symbols. X or Square goes through the suggested phrases or names, if there are any. Y or Triangle reads what you've typed. A or Cross accepts and B or Circle cancels.

Gamepad, the actions menu

It opens by holding the View button, or Create or Share on PlayStation, and pressing the right trigger, or R2. It is a list with everything that is a key on the keyboard and has no button of its own on the gamepad. Only the actions that apply at that moment are listed.

  • Order suspects to halt. Only on foot.
  • Shout to scare off pedestrians. Only on foot.
  • Speak in character, to those nearby.
  • Describe what your character is doing.
  • Write a message to the other players.
  • What the player next to you is wearing, and their profile. Only on foot.
  • Pull another player out of their vehicle. Only on foot.
  • Rob the pedestrian next to you. Only on foot.
  • Take cover, or leave it. Only on foot.
  • Slow time: turn it on or off.
  • Lock onto anyone, not only enemies.
  • Weapons list.
  • Use a first aid kit.
  • Your substances.
  • Your dog: have it search by scent. Only on foot.
  • Your dog: its menu. Only on foot.
  • Straighten the vehicle toward the nearest cardinal point. Only at the wheel.
  • Fish at sea, or siren on emergency vehicles.
  • Mission or job objective.
  • Back to the mission objective: GPS and radar.
  • Recover the last destination you removed from the GPS.
  • List of places, to mark a destination.
  • Map explorer.
  • Description of the place or neighborhood you're in.
  • Where your vehicle is, and mark it on the GPS.
  • Where am I, with coordinates.
  • Change the radar filter.
  • Repeat the last message.
  • Go to the first message in the history.
  • Go to the last message in the history.
  • Switch between assisted and free driving.
  • Switch on-foot movement between compass and turns.
  • GPS sound beacon: turn it on or off.
  • Quick save.
  • Turn the music down.
  • Turn the music up.
  • Put a mark in the session log.

In the server panel

The panel is the window for running your own online server. It is in the game's servidor folder.

  • F5: starts the server.
  • Shift F5: stops the server, warning the players and saving everything.
  • Control F5: restarts the server.
  • Control P: settings.
  • Control Q: leave the panel. If the server is running, it asks and stops it first.
  • Control N: announcement to all players.
  • Control M: message to the player chosen in the list.
  • Control E: kick the chosen player.
  • Control I: create an invitation and copy it to the clipboard.
  • Control D: change the message of the day.
  • Control O: go to the field for typing a command.
  • F6: refresh the status and the lists.
  • Control S: save now.
  • Control B: backup now.
  • Control R: restore a backup.
  • Control G: save the log to a file.
  • F1: open the server guide.
  • Control H: explains the control you are on.
  • Control Tab and Control Shift Tab: switch tab.
  • Alt plus the underlined letter: go to a control on the tab or open a menu.
  • Applications key or Shift F10: in the lists of players and accounts, actions on the chosen item.
